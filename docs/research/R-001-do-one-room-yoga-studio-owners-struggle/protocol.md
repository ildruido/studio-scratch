# R-001 Do one-room yoga studio owners struggle with bookings: protocol

Pre-registered before any search. After the first search, only the **Deviations** section changes. Method: `.claude/packs/research/FRAMEWORK.md`.

- **Type:** user reality check (branch `user-reality-check.md` of the research skill)
- **Tier:** standard. The product (a booking page for a small yoga studio) rests on this problem existing, so a wrong answer is expensive.
- **Decision this informs:** whether to keep "owners of one-room yoga studios" and "bookings" as the product's target and job, before the product foundation is written.
- **If the answer is yes we will…; if no we will…:** Yes: write the product foundation and personas on these users and this job. No (or mixed): reshape (another job such as payments or class packs, or a narrower segment) before writing them.
- **Sponsor:** the App Creator (Ale). **Lead:** Claude.
- **Kill criteria approved by the App Creator:** not required at this tier.

## Declared priors

- Source: the app's discovery profile only (product: a booking page for a small yoga studio; users: owners of one-room yoga studios). `starting-idea.md` is empty and principles are not set.
- General knowledge, not checked: many small studios already use all-in-one tools (Mindbody, Momence, Acuity, Glofox, Walla, Wix or Squarespace add-ons). I expect owners to complain more about cost, no-shows and admin than about "bookings" as such, and to be reasonably served by existing tools. I lean towards "Mixed".
- The product was named before any evidence, so I treat it as a hypothesis, not a plan.

## Hypotheses

U1 to U4 are key. Threshold and numbers are fixed here.

| Id | Key? | Hypothesis | Null or strongest alternative | Supported if | Kill criterion (contradicted if) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U1 Exists | yes | Independent one-room yoga studios exist in meaningful numbers in the launch markets. | Too few or too scattered. | A credible industry or official source puts independent or small yoga studios at 10,000 or more across the launch markets, at medium confidence. | Credible sources put the number below 10,000. No usable size source is Insufficient evidence, not a trigger. |
| U2 Does the job | yes | Owners (or the sole teacher-owner) handle bookings themselves. | Staff, a manager or an outside service handle it, or classes run without booking. | Voices from 3 or more independent communities describe handling bookings themselves. | Most evidence shows the job is handed to others or skipped. |
| U3 Job hurts | yes | Bookings cost owners meaningful time, money or results, raised without prompting. | A minor annoyance next to their real problems (finding students, income, burnout). | Unprompted complaints about booking, scheduling, no-shows or cancellations from 3 or more independent communities. | Pain appears mainly in vendor material or when prompted, or ranks low among owners' own listed problems in at least 2 independent communities. |
| U4 Workarounds fall short | yes | They use tools or manual methods and the same shortcomings recur. | Existing tools are good enough. | The same shortcoming (cost, complexity, fees, rigid features) recurs across 3 or more independent communities. | Satisfaction with current tools outweighs complaints in independent sources. |
| U5 Angle matters | no | The product's distinctive angle matters. | They want something generic. | n/a: principles are not set, so no angle exists yet. Expected Insufficient evidence. | Evidence of clear indifference to a simple, small-studio booking page. |
| U6 Would pay | no | They already pay for comparable help. | They do not pay. | Independent evidence that small studios pay for booking software. | Independent evidence they do not pay for comparable help. Expected Insufficient evidence or a light signal. |

Also asked neutrally: what are one-room studio owners' biggest problems, in their own words, and where does booking rank?

## Scope

- **Population:** owners or owner-teachers of independent studios with one teaching room (about 1 to 3 teachers). Excluded: franchises, gyms, multi-room studios, freelance teachers renting rooms with no studio of their own (logged as look-alikes).
- **Geography:** US, UK, Canada, Australia (assumed launch markets: the App Creator has not settled them; Ale to confirm or change).
- **Time window:** 2021 onward (post-pandemic booking habits); older only as context.
- **Languages:** English.
- **Include / exclude rules:** vendor material (booking software blogs and surveys) is a lead only and never carries a claim. Posts by consultants or vendors speaking for owners are excluded. Voices must come from owners or teachers who say they run a studio.

## Search plan

| Hypothesis | Source types | Neutral queries | Disconfirming queries | Looking for the missing (quiet, quit, failed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U1 | Industry statistics, trade bodies, official business counts | "number of yoga studios United States", "yoga studios UK count", "independent yoga studio market size" | "yoga studio industry consolidation decline", "yoga studios closing 2023 2024" | Closed studios |
| U2 | Reddit (r/yogateachers, r/smallbusiness), studio-owner forums, podcasts and interviews | "how do you manage bookings small yoga studio", "yoga studio owner day to day tasks" | "yoga studio hire front desk manager bookings", "studio owner outsource admin" | Owners who use staff |
| U3 | Same communities, plus teacher groups | "yoga studio owner biggest challenges", "yoga studio no-shows late cancellations" | "yoga studio owner biggest problem is students not booking", "yoga studio owners burnout income" | Owners who closed |
| U4 | Same, plus review sites read as voices, not as evidence of size | "yoga studio booking software complaints", "switching from Mindbody small studio" | "yoga studio booking software happy with", "easy booking setup small studio satisfied" | Owners who dropped a tool, owners who use paper or Instagram DMs |
| U5, U6 | Same | "small yoga studio pay booking software cost per month" | "yoga teacher free booking tool instead of paying" | none planned |

## Type-specific checks

- U1 size threshold: 10,000 studios across US, UK, Canada and Australia; at least one grade A source needed for medium confidence.
- Independence for U2 to U4: at least three communities that are different platforms, countries or niches (two subreddits count as one platform unless the posts differ by country or niche; a vendor's customer-story page never counts).
- Planned search for those who quit or failed: studio closures, and owners who abandoned a booking tool and why.

## Time box

20 minutes of searching and logging, then stop and report what is left.
