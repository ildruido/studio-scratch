# Design alternatives: booking page

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/W3wKzEa7zCsEk2krQuDsar (file: `docs/discovery/prototypes/design-alternatives-board.html`).

## What the rejected version taught
Ale tried the earlier version and found it generic. No earlier version is indexed in `prototypes.md`, so the details are not recorded.

## The three alternatives
- **A, The week at a glance:** timetable grid, tap, book. Small effort. Closest to the generic look.
- **B, Just tell me the next class:** one question, one suggested class, one-tap booking. Medium effort. Fastest for regulars.
- **C, Pick your mat:** the page is the studio floor; choose a class, then tap your mat. Medium to large effort. Only fits a one-room studio.

## Pick (Ale, 2026-11-26)
**C.** Kept: B's "your usual" shortcut for returning students; A's week strip at the top.
Parked as ideas, not scope: owner floor-plan editor (first version uses one fixed layout), bring a friend, mat rental, waitlist, add to calendar, cancel link.

## Not checked
Not tried on a real phone. No studio owner or student has seen it. Whether students care about choosing a spot is an unproven guess; the board proves nothing about demand.
